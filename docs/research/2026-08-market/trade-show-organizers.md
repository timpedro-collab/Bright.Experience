# Trade-Show & Exhibition Organizers: Ecosystem Research for Bright.Experience

Research date: 2 Aug 2026. Scope: UK/EU/US trade-show and exhibition organizers, and the exhibitor/sponsor-facing platforms they use. All claims are sourced from live pages, PDFs, help-desk articles and prospectuses fetched during research; URLs inline.

---

## 1. Informa (Informa Markets / Informa Connect / Informa Festivals)

**Scale & structure.** World's largest organizer. Three relevant divisions: Informa Markets (trade shows), Informa Connect (conferences), and Informa Festivals — a new division created 1 Jan 2025 after the £1.2bn Ascential acquisition, housing Cannes Lions, Money20/20, Finovate, Black Hat, GDC and London Tech Week ([Informa company update PDF](https://www.informa.com/globalassets/documents/investor-relations/2024/informa-company-update-on-ascential-completion.pdf), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/deals/informa-agrees-16-billion-deal-cannes-lions-owner-ascential-2024-07-24/)). Also acquired Tarsus (160+ events incl. Labelexpo) for $940M in 2023.

**How exhibitors buy.** Sales-led, slow: exhibitor submits an enquiry form on the event site ("Become an Exhibitor"), waits for a sales rep, receives a Booking Form; a binding contract only forms when Informa sends written acceptance (SPEX T&Cs: [Informa Markets USA Beauty T&Cs](https://www.informamarkets.com/content/dam/Informa/informa-markets/usa-beauty/Clean_USAB_Version_Informa_Markets_USAB_SPEX_TsCs_FINAL_(USA)_(April2025).pdf)). Pricing is not published — "Pricing varies between show brand and booth options... email us" ([MD&M Midwest exhibitor FAQ](https://informa-stage65.adobecqms.net/content/informa/mdmmidwest/en/exhibit/exhibitor-faqs.html)). Third-party reporting notes enquiries can take *weeks* to be processed ([eventtipsglobal.com](https://eventtipsglobal.com/is-it-worth-exhibiting-with-informa-markets/)). Notably, the Booking Form embeds Sponsorship in the same "Package" definition as space — sponsorship is contractually one bundle with the booth.

**Exhibitor portal stack.** Fragmented but deep:
- **Exhibitor Hub** (exhibitor-hub.informaconnect.com) — a Freshdesk-style knowledge base per show family.
- **Informa Connect Tech Events app** — exhibitor console: virtual company page, badge scanning, per-event lead-qualification surveys built in an "Exhibitor Center," lead export ([FAQ](https://exhibitor-hub.informaconnect.com/support/solutions/articles/150000230551-the-informa-connect-tech-events-app-faq)).
- **Lead Insights** — a *cross-event* analytics platform: month-by-month trends attributed to individual events, firmographics, "most engaged accounts," Leads vs Net New Leads columns, segment builder, master CSV export to CRM ([Insights & reporting](https://exhibitor-hub.informaconnect.com/support/solutions/articles/150000206160-lead-insights-insights-and-reporting)). This is the most sophisticated exhibitor-ROI product found among organizers: it treats every show as a "campaign" in a portfolio.
- Notable friction the org itself documents: lead-qualification answers do **not** flow into Lead Insights (data silo), and registration is required even for non-attending admins.
- Marketing upsell: free "Exhibitor Invites Program" (personalized promo code, banners, ready-made web invites) delivered in the Exhibitor Portal (MD&M FAQ above).

**Sponsorship packaging.** Per-show prospectuses and consultative sale; e.g. Labelexpo/LOUPE (ex-Tarsus, now Informa) publishes a "3 simple steps" funnel — download brochure → talk to team → become a sponsor — with inventory like entrance signage, floor graphics, badges/lanyards, aisle banners, seminars ([LOUPE Americas sponsorships](https://www.loupe-americas.com/sponsorship-opportunities)).

## 2. RX Global (Reed Exhibitions)

**Exhibitor tooling is bundled, not optional.** "Every exhibitor now has a core set of RX digital tools included in their smart event package": searchable directory listing, **Lead Manager App** (formerly Emperia) badge scanning, and **Exhibitor Dashboard** ([Four digital tools](https://rxglobal.com/four-digital-tools-boost-trade-show-sales)).

**Exhibitor Dashboard** (the flagship): real-time consolidation of leads collected, profile views, digital engagement and matchmaking recommendations; nudges exhibitors to follow up neglected leads; highlights profile-completion %; positions itself explicitly as ROI-justification tooling ("provide clear evidence of the value your participation delivered") ([RX real-time data article](https://rxglobal.com/using-real-time-data-and-analytics-drive-event-success)). A paid **PRO version benchmarks exhibitors against leading/average companies in their category** ([How RX tech powers customer success](https://rxglobal.com/how-rx-tech-powers-customer-success)). Rolled out globally from a 20-company pilot in Brazil; >30% of exhibitors at some shows review their performance.

**Upsell inventory (self-descriptive, tiered):**
- **Premium Directory Listings** — Silver = double-height listing + product carousel + callback requests; Gold = home-page banner ads + product-category sponsorship, described as "the digital equivalent of having the biggest stand inside the show entrance" ([RX Digital & Data](https://rxglobal.com/rx-digital-data-0)).
- **Lead Booster / digital incentives** — exhibitor-created single-page offers (prize draws, samples, meet-and-greets) delivered to visitors' phones as a "digital gift bag" per show. This is engagement-as-inventory: RX monetizes the *incentive channel* itself.
- Digital package upgrades (Bronze/Silver/Gold) sold in-portal at e.g. MIP London Company Hub ([user guide PDF](https://www.mip-london.com/content/dam/sitebuilder/rm/mip-london/2026/pdf/MIPLONDON26_CompanyHub_AdminUserGuide.pdf)).

**Ops innovation:** Exhibitor WhatsApp Groups (born in Brazil) as low-cost, high-trust exhibitor comms channel — acknowledged failure of email/manual comms.

## 3. Clarion Events

Sales-led, "concierge" positioning. Clarion Gift Shows publishes a "Service Promise": a dedicated **Exhibit Service Manager** ("show concierge"), a Key Accounts rep who recruits named buyers exhibitors want to see, matchmaking service tiers, and a sales lead "who will always call you back" ([service promise](https://clariongiftandsouvenir.com/service-promise)). Clarion Gaming routes exhibit/advertise interest to "B2B solutions team" scheduling calls ([clariongaming.com/exhibit-advertise](https://www.clariongaming.com/exhibit-advertise)).

**Sponsorship packaging (FDIC International is the best example):** thought-leadership-led inventory — buy a 90-minute classroom session as "official presenter," sponsor vetted curriculum sessions, "Program Title Sponsor" flagship combining opening-program visibility + venue-wide branding; plus event signs, listing upgrades, eblasts to pre-registered attendees, app sponsorship, podcasts, custom onsite video shared post-show ([sponsor page](https://clarionfirerescue.com/events/fdic/sponsor/), [advertise page](https://clarionfirerescue.com/events/fdic/advertise/)). Their advertise enquiry form is a long checkbox list (Converge+ Enterprise, webcasts, microsites, audience extension, surveys) — media-agency-style cross-sell of the whole portfolio, and booth discounts for bundling ad/sponsorship spend.

## 4. Hyve Group

**Meetings-first pivot.** Hyve rebuilt its flagship shows around facilitated, double-opt-in meetings programmes: **Connect @ Bett** delivered 7,000 meetings across 2,300 people from 1,500 orgs in year one and is called "the primary way to build strategic relationships at Bett" ([Hyve news](https://hyve.group/news/2023/bett-successfully-launches-ground-breaking-new-meetings-programme-connect-@-bett/)). Group-wide, Hyve facilitates 160,000+ meetings/year and in 2026 moved flagship events (Shoptalk family, Groceryshop, Fintech Meetup, POSSIBLE, Bett, CWIEME) onto **ExpoPlatform** for matchmaking and "multi-format meeting orchestration" ([exhibitoronline](https://www.exhibitoronline.com/news/article.asp?ID=25271)). Shoptalk's "Meetup" sells *guaranteed* 1:1 meetings with pre-vetted decision-makers — outcome-denominated inventory, the closest thing in the market to Bright's "measured to the play" logic but for meetings rather than brand engagement.

**Bett partnerships brochure** (2024 PDF, [cdn.asp.events](https://cdn.asp.events/CLIENT_Ascentia_4E961A52_5056_B739_54289B84DF34E888/sites/bett-show-uk-2024/media/PDFs/Bett-2024-Partnerships-Brochure.pdf)) lists deliverables à la carte: dedicated stand, content-session seats, digital ads on event screens, newsletter/social inclusions, community-hub interviews — "bespoke agreements" via named partnership managers.

## 5. Easyfairs

**The most productized exhibitor offer in the market.** **EasyGo** is a three-tier visibility + lead-gen package (GoLeads / GoPlus / GoPremium) layered on every stand ([easyfairs.com/easygo](https://www.easyfairs.com/easygo/), [2025 packages PDF](https://www.easyfairs.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/EasyGo-Packages_2025.pdf)):
- GoLeads: invite tool with real-time arrival alerts, AI matchmaking in the app, 1 **Smart Badge reader** (visitors tap their NFC badge on the exhibitor's reader → full contact details exchanged automatically, "even if you never spoke"), Visit Connect badge-scanning web app.
- GoPlus: +1 Smart Badge reader, highlighted logo, video profile header, extra Innovation Gallery entry; marketed as "X% more leads than GoLeads."
- GoPremium: 3 readers, logo on the entrance ticket and every registration step, logo/ad in high-traffic venue areas.
- Everything is managed in **My Easyfairs**, "your single dashboard for invitations, content, badge scanners and post-show data. Save hours of admin and prove ROI fast."

Package upgrade ladder quantified by expected lead uplift is exactly the pattern Bright can copy for machine tiers. Pricing is per-event and account-manager-gated (friction: no self-serve checkout).

## 6. Emerald (US)

Portfolio of 140+ US shows. Exhibitor Dashboard is "a one-stop shop": profile, **explore sponsorship opportunities**, product listings, invoices, insurance uploads, with one login persisted across all Emerald events ([ASD Market Week dashboard](https://asdonline.com/exhibitor-dashboard/)). Its networking/lead layer ("My Team", built on Grip) qualifies **Inbound Leads** — attendees who expressed interest in the company, products, content or staff — into Contacts via accepted meetings or mutual interest, with badge-scan scoring and master exports ([Emerald ERC user guide PDF](http://www.emeraldx.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Emerald-Exhibitor-Resource-Center-User-Guides.pdf)). IWCE's portal includes "Complete Sponsorship Deliverables — stay on top of tasks related to your sponsorship benefits" ([iwceexpo.com](https://iwceexpo.com/exhibitor-resources/)) — sponsorship fulfilment as an in-portal task list. Enterprise Connect runs a free "Co-Marketing Program" where registrations via an exhibitor's promo code earn rewards ([enterpriseconnect.com](https://enterpriseconnect.com/exhibitor-resource-center/)). **Emerald Xcelerator** is a division for launching new brands/experiences ([Nasdaq PR](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/emerald-announces-new-division-dedicated-to-driving-growth-through-new-brand)).

## 7. Messe Frankfurt

The German Messe model: venue+organizer with an industrialized service e-commerce layer.
- **Shop for Exhibitor Services** (serviceshop.messefrankfurt.com): electricity, water, cleaning, security, logistics, parking, catering — with **25% express surcharges** for late orders and hard order deadlines ([ISH shops page](https://ish.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/planning-preparation/exhibitors/shops.html), [Heimtextil](https://heimtextil.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/exhibitor-services/order-services.html)). Deadline-pricing is a deliberate revenue/ops lever.
- **Media Package Manager (MPM)** — separate tool to publish company/product data across print, online exhibitor search, Navigator app, interactive hall plan.
- **Fairconstruction stand configurator**, **Ticket Services** portal (free pass quotas auto-calculated from stand size; only used tickets invoiced), **Promotion Shop** for invite materials, plus **LeadSuccess** lead capture (Ambiente service documentation PDF).
- Friction: at least five separate logins/tools per show; the shop for a 2027 show "will be available from mid-January 2027" — long dead periods.

## 8. Koelnmesse

Same model: **Service Shop** unlocked once the booth is confirmed (services directly orderable or RFQ-based), a separate **media shop** for print/online advertising, ticket-code ordering where only used tickets are invoiced ([imm cologne order services](https://www.imm-cologne.com/for-exhibitors/order-services/), [ISM exhibitor passes](https://www.ism-cologne.com/for-exhibitors/order-services/exhibitor-passes/)). Corporate-level **Sponsoring** page sells "eye-catching display areas, thematic partnerships, or tailor-made activations... reach your target groups where they least expect it" with a named Sponsoring Sales Manager ([koelnmesse.com sponsoring](https://www.koelnmesse.com/location-and-services/services/sponsoring/)); advertising spaces include megaprints, floor stickers, LED screens, promotion teams, even zeppelins ([advertising spaces](https://www.koelnmesse.com/location-and-services/services/advertising-spaces/)). "Tailor-made activations" are sold with zero productization — no catalog, no pricing, no performance data: pure contact-the-team.

## 9. Messe München

**Exhibitor Shop** per event; the application books *only* the space — everything else (stand build, tech, furniture, passes, customer vouchers where only redeemed vouchers are billed) comes later through the shop, with a documented main-contact → delegated-authorizations flow ([messe-muenchen.de exhibitor shops](https://messe-muenchen.de/en/services/exhibitor-shops/), [f.re.e application](https://free-muenchen.de/en/trade-fair/exhibitors/application-prices/)). Media sales are outsourced to jl.medien with **published rate cards**: e.g. electronica exhibitor-directory "exclusive welcome interstitial" €7,500, leaderboard €4,500, quick link €850; f.re.e Ad Specials Gold/Silver/Bronze bundles print+digital ([electronica media services](https://www.electronica-media.de/Marketing-Services/2026/en/Media-Services/Online/Exhibitor-directory-exclusive-placements), [f.re.e media services](https://www.free-mediaservices.de/Marketing-Services/2026/en/Advertisements-Digital-advertising/Exhibitor-portal)). Free stage slots (talks, prize draws) are offered to exhibitors as engagement inventory at consumer shows.

All year round": Lead Scanner/Lead Scanner Plus, 1:1 messaging, push notifications to selected audiences, digital agenda/staff, Event & Live Event (webinars), Digital Logo on the official map, in-pavilion ad totems — active 365 days ([fieramilano.it/digital-platform](https://www.fieramilano.it/en/digital-platform.html)). ## 11. Comexposium

## 12. Web Summit

**The most e-commerce-like sponsor funnel found.** Partners buy stands/packages sales-led, but *add-ons run through an actual Shopify storefront* — [shop.websummit.com](https://shop.websummit.com/pages/add-ons): ticket bundles, digital branding (The Cube, stage banners, outdoor screens), printed branding (gangway banners, column wraps — "no longer available, speak to your account manager"), masterclasses priced by capacity (60/100/150 pax), meeting rooms by size (4–12 pax). The Partner Store also sells all event services (electrics, AV, rigging, furniture, labour) with a hard open/close window and **no onsite orders** ([WS22 partner hub](https://sites.google.com/websummit.com/ws22-partnerhub/general-information)). # Exhibitor-facing platforms (the software layer organizers buy)

## Map Your Show (MYS)

Full-stack US trade-show platform (used by CES among others; "trusted by 1.7M exhibitors"). Key modules ([mapyourshow.com](https://www.mapyourshow.com/)):
- **Floor Builder** — drag-and-drop floorplan with Max Fit compliance auditing and auto-renumbering.
- **Booth & Sponsorship Sales** — exhibitors compare rates and buy space *directly from the interactive floor plan*; digital-ad and sponsorship opportunities are embedded **within the space-application flow**; online signatures; automated invoicing; sales cycles for renewals ([module sheet](https://info.mapyourshow.com/booth-and-sponsorship-sales)).
- **Exhibitor Resource Center** — portal landing page, **task checklist with categories, vendor-linked tasks and automated deadline-reminder emails**, balance payment, lead stats, SSO across registration/housing/show services, and in-portal purchase of advertising/sponsorships ([ERC page](https://www.mapyourshow.com/exhibitor-resource-center)).
- **Sales Accelerator** — AI to identify top leads and draft personalized follow-ups; **MYS Insights** — AI predicting event sales and flagging at-risk exhibitors for organizers.

## A2Z Events (Personify)

Launched **A2Z Event Sales Engine** Oct 2025 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/personify-launches-a2z-event-sales-engine-to-transform-how-event-professionals-drive-revenue-and-engage-exhibitors-302575993.html)): booths + sponsorships + add-ons in **one cart, one checkout** inside the Event Portal. The **Sponsorship Gallery** presents sponsorship inventory "in a familiar online-store format" with live pricing, real-time remaining-inventory counts, filter-by-budget; organizers can send **direct links that drop specific sponsorship items straight into an exhibitor's cart** ([A2Z blog](https://mya2zevents.com/blog/a2z-events-sales-engine/)). Their EDC add-on ties **purchased sponsorship items to fulfilment tasks with custom forms** assigned in the portal ([Zendesk guide](https://a2zevents.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/35190095284493-EDC-Sales-Comprehensive-Overview-Setup-Guide)). Claim: "within the first week, more than half of the floor can be sold" self-serve.

## ExpoFP

Budget interactive-floorplan vendor. Exhibitors buy booths by credit card directly on the map; organizers upsell Featured Listings, header logos, in-booth logos with self-set prices; auto-login links replace an exhibitor portal; API embeds the plan into any registration flow ([expofp.com](https://expofp.com/), [FAQ](https://expofp.com/faq)). Sales pitch: "most organizers recover the platform cost before the event opens" by selling floorplan sponsorship inventory.

## ExpoPlatform

AI event-tech used by Informa, Comexposium, Hyve, CloserStill. Monetization toolkit: sell profile headers, virtual lobby branding, app splash screens, banners/pop-ups across web+mobile+floorplan; premium showroom profiles and search-priority as package levers; **premium real-time statistics dashboards sold to sponsors** (impressions, clicks, pop-up engagement, full export); "Lead Intelligence" claimed +250% leads vs traditional retrieval; 365 Marketplace for year-round sponsor leads ([sponsorship monetization](https://expoplatform.com/sponsorship-monetization/), [sponsor statistics helpdesk](https://help.expoplatform.com/admin-home/sponsor-statistics-for-organiser)).

## Freeman + Clarity Media (services & sponsorship marketplace layer)

Freeman launched a consumer-grade **exhibitor services e-commerce platform** (Dec 2025): personalized planning checklist, curated per-show Shopping Guide (addressing "booth FOMO" by recommending upgrades), real-time shipping/invoice visibility, online Material Handling Agreements ([Freeman PR](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/freeman-launches-exhibitor-services-platform-151500424.html), [TSNN demo](https://www.tsnn.com/exhibitors/exclusive-tsnn-demos-freeman-s-new-exhibitor-services-platform-at-iaee-s-expo-expo-)). Simultaneously partnered with **Clarity Media Partners** on an **AI-powered sponsorship marketplace** automating the sponsorship lifecycle — discovery, inventory management, sales, fulfilment — with one-week deployment and real-time analytics on "inventory performance, deal velocity, and fulfillment status" ([exhibitoronline](https://www.exhibitoronline.com/news/article.asp?ID=24844)). **This is the most direct signal that sponsorship inventory is becoming a marketplace category — and it's a natural distribution rail for Bright machines as listable, fulfillable inventory items.**

## Adjacent: measurable-engagement vendors exhibitors buy directly

- **Captello** — "event-to-revenue intelligence": 60+ branded digital game activations (prize wheels, whack-a-mole, trivia) fused with universal lead capture, scoring, CRM routing and pipeline attribution ("Revelation" ties every game play to attributed pipeline) ([captello.com](https://www.captello.com/)). Digital-only; validates games-as-lead-gen with hard attribution, the exact claim Bright makes for physical machines.
- **VenuIQ** — passive Bluetooth-beacon attendee tracking that gives sponsors named-person booth visits + dwell time, sold explicitly as renewal-protection for organizers ([venu-iq.com](https://venu-iq.com/how-to-prove-event-roi-to-sponsors-and-exhibitors/)).
- **Bizzabo** — dedicated sponsor portal where sponsors self-serve their own lead data and brand-visibility stats in real time; preaches sponsor-level (not aggregate) reporting delivered within days ([bizzabo blog](https://www.bizzabo.com/blog/how-to-prove-sponsor-roi-events)).
- **Physical game-machine rental agencies** (Made for Arcade, Elation Digital, Trade Show Labs, Snap Entertainment) sell branded claw machines with badge-scan-gated plays *directly to exhibitors* — none are sold **through organizers as packaged sponsorship inventory** ([madeforarcade.com](https://madeforarcade.com/fully-branded-claw-machine-for-trade-shows-brand-activations-the-ultimate-b2b-event-attraction/), [tradeshowlabs.com](https://www.tradeshowlabs.com/custom-branded-claw-machine-for-trades-shows)). This channel gap is Bright's organizer-portal thesis, confirmed.

## Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Two funnel archetypes.** (a) Sales-led opacity: Informa, Clarion, Hyve, Money20/20, Koelnmesse sponsoring — enquiry form → rep → bespoke package; no public pricing; weeks of latency. (b) Self-serve commerce: A2Z Sales Engine, MYS, ExpoFP, Web Summit's Shopify add-on store, Messe service shops — carted, priced, deadline-driven. The industry is visibly migrating from (a) to (b) for everything *except* high-value sponsorship, which stays consultative.
2. **Space is the anchor; everything else is upsell.** The application/contract covers only square meters; services, media, visibility and lead tools are separate revenue layers (Messe München states this explicitly). Organizers engineer upsell moments: embedded sponsorship offers inside the space application (MYS), cart-drop links (A2Z), express surcharges for late orders (Messe Frankfurt +25%), early-bird price ladders.
3. **Lead capture became the packaging spine.** Easyfairs (EasyGo tiers = number of Smart Badge readers), RX (Lead Manager bundled with every stand), Emerald (Inbound Leads), Fiera Milano (Lead Scanner). Tiers are marketed by *expected lead uplift* ("X% more leads than GoLeads").
4. **Exhibitor ROI dashboards are now table stakes at the majors** — RX Exhibitor Dashboard (with PRO benchmarking), Informa Lead Insights (cross-event portfolio analytics), Emerald My Team — but they measure *leads and profile views*, not brand engagement, queues, dwell or play-level interaction.
5. **Sponsorship inventory is still mostly static media**: signage, lanyards, banners, interstitials, session sponsorship, hospitality. Published price ladders exist (CES, NRF APAC $1k push notification → $60k Welcome Hub, electronica €850–€7,500). "Custom activations" appear on nearly every prospectus (CES, Koelnmesse "tailor-made activations", BIT "experiential activations", Money20/20 show-floor activations) but are always contact-sales, bespoke, unpriced, and **never performance-quantified**.
6. **Proof-of-performance pressure is rising industry-wide.** Organizer-side thought leadership (Bizzabo, VenuIQ, CrowdComms, Bridged) converges on: sponsor-level (not aggregate) reports, delivered within 5 business days, with attributable engagement → pipeline. Exhibitor budgets are shrinking (75% expect fewer dollars in 2025-26; show-services costs up 38% since 2019 — Evolio/Lippman VOE survey [PDF](https://cdn.asp.events/CLIENT_Lippman__27AF112E_5056_B733_4905CC1658AA5A58/sites/lippman-connects-2023/media/libraries/resources-1/VOE-Voice-of-the-Exhibitor-EVOLIO-ESR-June-2025.pdf)), so every spend must be defensible.
7. **Exhibitor-portal friction is a documented churn driver**: repeated data entry, 100-page manuals, organizer→venue→supplier ping-pong ([TSNN churn piece](https://www.tsnn.com/event-management/why-are-we-making-exhibiting-so-hard-and-what-s-behind-our-high-exhibitor-churn)); best practice is task-based portals opened 14+ weeks out with phased deadlines (infosalons guide). The fix pattern everywhere is checklists + reminders + status visibility — exactly Bright's customer-portal readiness model.

## What they do better than Bright.Experience (as described)

1. **Cross-event portfolio analytics (Informa Lead Insights).** Brands exhibiting at many shows get month-by-month, event-attributed trend lines, firmographics and net-new-lead accounting across the whole portfolio. Bright's telemetry is per-event; repeat brand customers have no portfolio view.
2. **Competitive benchmarking as a paid tier (RX Dashboard PRO).** "Compare yourself against the leading and average companies in your category" is both an upsell and a performance-improvement loop. Bright reports absolute numbers but (as described) no benchmark context ("your machine did 2.4x median plays for a 3-day tech show").
3. **Single-cart commerce for complex inventory (A2Z Sales Engine, MYS).** Booth + sponsorships + add-ons in one checkout, with direct cart-drop links sales reps can text to a buyer. Bright's quote-intake → proposal flow is still fundamentally quote-led.
4. **Deadline economics (Messe Frankfurt).** Express surcharges and early-bird ladders convert operational deadlines into both revenue and on-time behavior. Bright's logistics/creative deadlines are (as described) tracked but not price-enforced.
5. **Behavior-triggering nudges (RX).** The dashboard "nudges you to follow up your leads if you neglect to do so" and scores profile completion. Activation-grade nudging beats passive reporting.
6. **Guaranteed-outcome inventory (Hyve/Shoptalk Meetup).** Selling *guaranteed meetings with pre-vetted buyers* reframes the purchase from access to outcome. Bright sells measured outcomes but not guaranteed ones.
7. **Buyer-enablement collateral (Money20/20 "Build your business case", CES ROI flyers).** Pages designed to arm an internal champion to defend the spend to their CFO.
8. **Task-linked fulfilment (A2Z EDC, Emerald IWCE).** Each purchased sponsorship item spawns a fulfilment task with a custom form in the portal — purchase and delivery are one object.

## Gaps Bright could fill

1. **No organizer sells turnkey, measured physical activations.** Every prospectus has a "custom activations" line; none has a catalog item with hardware, logistics, staffing, live telemetry and a 24h proof-of-performance report. Claw/arcade machines reach shows only via exhibitor-direct rental agencies with no organizer margin, no data integration, no sponsorship packaging. An organizer-ready "activation SKU" (machine + placement + expected plays/leads + revenue share) is whitespace.
2. **Sponsorship prospectuses carry zero performance data.** CES prints prices but not expected impressions/engagement; NRF prints $10k aisle banners with no metrics. Bright's organizer portal already has "sponsor pitch links with expected-performance data" — that artifact type doesn't exist anywhere else in the market and should be loudly marketed.
3. **Engagement inventory is missing from exhibitor upsell stores.** Freeman's shopping guide sells carpet and AV; Messe shops sell electricity and plants; nobody's exhibitor store sells "a crowd-pulling machine with lead capture" as an orderable line item next to furniture. Slotting Bright machines into GSC/venue service shops and organizer resource centers is an untapped channel.
4. **Sponsor-level live dashboards are rare on the organizer side.** ExpoPlatform sells sponsors a stats dashboard for *banner clicks*; VenuIQ needs beacons on every lanyard. A physical machine that natively produces named-lead + play + queue telemetry gives organizers sponsor-level live proof with zero extra infrastructure.
5. **Renewal ammunition.** Organizers' #1 commercial anxiety (per VenuIQ, Bizzabo, CrowdComms content) is proving sponsor ROI fast enough to protect renewals. Bright's 24h board-ready report is precisely renewal ammunition — position it to organizers as "sponsorship retention infrastructure," not as a game.
6. **Marketplace listing rails are being built right now.** Freeman/Clarity's AI sponsorship marketplace (launched Dec 2025) wants fulfilment partners. Bright should be a listed fulfilment/inventory partner: machines as discoverable, purchasable, fulfillable sponsorship items.
7. **Dead-time monetization.** Web Summit's store closes 5 weeks before the show with "no onsite orders"; Messe shops open only months before events. A machine that can be confirmed late (because Bright owns logistics) is a late-money product organizers currently can't sell.
