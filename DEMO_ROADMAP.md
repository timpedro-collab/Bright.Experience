## Act 1 — The premium first impression  *(public site)*

**Show:** Start logged out on the public site / `/catalog`. Open **Case Studies →
Costa Coffee — Catch‑A‑Matcha**. Scroll the hero, the photo gallery, the performance
grid, the demographic bars and the venue footprint; click **View the full case study**.

**Say:**
- "Everything a client touches looks like *this* — this is the bar we set before we even talk about software."
- "This is a real activation: **3,270 game plays, 200k brand impressions, ~2,000 opt‑ins at 100% consent**. The proof is the product."
- "Notice the report is on‑brand and shareable — that's generated by the platform, not built by hand."

## Act 2 — How the work comes in  *(quiz → proposal → book)*

**Show:** `/quiz` — answer 3–4 questions. Land on the recommendation/proposal. Walk the
proposal: the recommended package, the **reach model** (impressions / interactions / leads /
media value), and the **book / book a walkthrough** step.

**Say:**
- "A prospect self‑qualifies in under a minute — and we instantly show them *reach in numbers they care about*, not a price list."
- "That proposal is a live document, not a PDF. When they book, everything they entered flows straight into delivery — no re‑keying."

## Act 3 — The client journey  *(James Chen — Customer)*

**Show:** Sign in as **James Chen**. Land on the customer home — only **Coca‑Cola** events
appear (scoped to their account). Open **Coca‑Cola Summer Festival 2026**:
- The **event timeline / sections** with status colours.
- Open **Creative briefing** — it's marked complete *and the fields are filled in* (brand
  kit, colours/fonts, webform questions, prize details, onsite contact).
- Open **Assets** and **Approvals** to show uploaded creative and the approval state.

**Say:**
- "A customer only ever sees their own events — clean, scoped, no clutter."
- "Every section tells them exactly what's done and what's next. No 'where are we?' emails."
- "And critically — *complete means complete*. What they submitted here is the exact same record the delivery team works from. One source of truth."

> **Data‑consistency beat:** keep this brief open; you'll prove in Act 4 that the creative
> team sees the *identical* submitted data.

## Act 4 — The control room  *(Tim / Dan / Theo — internal)*

**Show:** Sign in as **Tim Pedro** (or **Dan Barnes**). Show the **focused home / attention

## Cheat sheet — quick answers

- **"Is this real data?"** — It's a fully seeded demo environment; the architecture (Supabase/Postgres, role‑based access, real‑time) is production‑shaped.
- **"Who can see what?"** — Role‑based access: customers see only their account; internal roles get delivery views; partners get a co‑branded slice.
- **"How fast to launch a new event?"** — Quiz/proposal → book auto‑creates the event with its task pipeline pre‑populated.
- **"What's next?"** — Link the events website to the landing page, finish the portal and connect it to **Connect** (brand self‑serve), and unlock short‑term activations beyond retail — festivals, stadiums, concerts (see slide 3).
