01 · The Activation

A live engagement play, measured on participation

Over five consecutive weekends, Costa took an interactive Bright.Blue activation to ten UK city centres, each sited in a high-footfall location directly outside a Costa Coffee store. Every interaction combined a branded game, a merchandise dispense and a product sample, gated to an adult (18+) audience. As an experiential format, success is measured on plays, sampling, consented data capture and audience quality, not on passive media reach.

Play, sample, share data

A giant matcha-cup installation drew crowds in, and the game turned a glance into a full hands-on interaction: play, win merchandise, sample the product, and opt in.

A touring footprint

Ten city centres in five weekends gave national reach from a single unit, testing the concept across very different high-street environments.

07 · Key Insights

Live trial at national scale

Across five weekends and ten city centres, the activation drove 3,270 hands-on interactions, each one a game played, a sample tried and a piece of merchandise won. The experience drew a predominantly female (67%) and family-aged crowd, with 35-44 the single largest group, and footfall peaked at 55 plays in a single hour. Sited directly outside a Costa Coffee store in each city centre, the installation delivered an estimated 200,000+ brand impressions to passing footfall. Crucially, 100% of customers who shared their details opted in to marketing, turning thousands of live product trials into a fully consented first-party database.

As an experiential activation, this is measured on engagement, sampling, audience quality and consented data capture, the metrics that matter for a live event format. It is the same hardware behind our DOOH and retail case studies, deployed against a different objective.
